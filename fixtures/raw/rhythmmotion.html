ODC Dance Commons, located in the Mission District of San Francisco, offers an environment where professionals, emerging artists, students, and dance fans can congregate, exchange ideas and inspire one another. A gathering place for the Bay Area cultural community since 2005, new art work is percolating in every studio, and 200 classes a week in all forms of dance draw hundreds of students of all ages each day. The gorgeous 33,000 square foot facility includes a total of seven fully-equipped studios, two performance spaces, a free health clinic for dancers, a community lounge, a Pilates center, gallery spaces, locker rooms, showers, and more.

Metered and 2-hour parking is available on adjacent streets. There are two pay parking garages, one behind Wells Fargo at 16th and Hoff St (Hoff runs parallel between Mission and Valencia St), and another at 2351 Mission St, past 19th street.

ODC School and the Rhythm & Motion Dance Program have partnered since 2005 to offer classes for everyone, from absolute beginners to professionals. Our classes are taught by an excellent professional faculty representing many dance disciplines. We believe that dance should be a visible, accessible, and joyful part of a larger world.

ODC Dance Commons also serves as Rhythm & Motion’s national headquarters. Please stop by our office on the ground floor across from Skirball Studio any time!
